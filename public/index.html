Install in a minute

Kuro isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow the install once.

  1. 1

    Download

    Tap Download for Android on your phone. If Chrome asks, choose Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Open the file

    Open kuro.apk from the notification or your Downloads. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.

  3. 3

    Install and read

    Tap Install, then Open. Kuro tells you inside the app when a new version is out.

Questions

Is Kuro really free?

Yes. Every chapter is free and there are no ads. Readers who want to support the servers can pledge on Patreon and get a gold crown next to their name. That's optional.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Kuro is for Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or newer).

Why does Android warn me about the file?

Android shows that warning for every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Only download Kuro from this page, so you always get the real app.

Do I need an account?

No. Your library and reading progress are saved on your phone. Pick a username only if you want to post in the community, and add a password if you want your account on another phone.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, Kuro shows "Kuro is here" when you open it. Tap it, download, and install over the old app: your library, downloads and account stay.

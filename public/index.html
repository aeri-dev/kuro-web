Features

Built for reading, not for ads

Reader

A reader that feels instant

No loading between pages: the next ones are already there, and the next chapter loads before you reach it. Pinch to zoom anywhere on a page, like in your gallery. Tap the sides to turn, the middle for controls.

  • Page by page or vertical scroll
  • Original or data-saver quality
  • Jump to any page, bookmark any page

Manhwa & webtoons

Scroll the way it's meant to be read

Webtoons open in vertical scroll. The bars slide away as you read, a tap on the right moves you down, and reaching the bottom opens the next chapter by itself.

  • Auto next chapter
  • Bars hide while you scroll
  • Comments and likes at the end of each chapter

Discover

Find your next obsession

Trending titles, all-time favorites, top manhwa, romance and new releases on the home screen, and search across 250,000+ titles by any spelling, with genre and type filters.

  • Chapters from several sources, picked for you

  • Switch source yourself any time

Library

FAQ

Questions

Is Kuro really free?

Yes. Every chapter is free and there are no ads. Readers who want to help pay for the servers can pledge on Patreon and get a gold crown next to their name. That's optional.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Kuro is for Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer.

Why does Android warn me about the file?

Android shows that warning for every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Only download Kuro from this page, so you always get the real app.

Do I need an account?

No. Your library and reading progress stay on your phone. Pick a username only if you want to post in the community, and add a password if you want your account on another phone.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, Kuro tells you when you open it. Download it and install over the old app: your library, downloads and account stay.
